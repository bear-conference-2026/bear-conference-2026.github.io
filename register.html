Submission Guidelines

We invite submissions from all disciplines (Sciences, Engineering, Medicine) that utilize BEAR services or High Performance Computing in their research.

Oral Presentations
  • Standard Talk: 15 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A. Ideal for completed research or significant milestones.
  • Lightning Talk: 5 minutes. Perfect for early-stage research or rapid methodology overviews.
Poster Presentations
  • Format: A0 or A1 Portrait.
  • Posters will be displayed in the Old Gym hall during lunch and coffee breaks.
  • Prizes will be awarded for "Best Poster" courtesy of Lenovo.
