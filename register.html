Submission Guidelines

We invite submissions from all disciplines that utilize BEAR services, High Performance Computing, and AI in their research. Presentations should incorporate responsible AI usage by embracing AI with Integrity and consider Ethical implications for AI. For more information, see the UKRIO guideline on embracing AI with integrity.

Oral Presentations
  • Standard Talk: 15 mins + 5 mins Q&A.
  • Lightning Talk: 5 mins perfect for early-stage research.
  • Prizes for "Best Talk" courtesy of Lenovo & NVIDIA.
Poster Presentations
  • Format: A1 Portrait.
  • Display in Old Gym hall during breaks.
  • Prizes for "Best Poster" courtesy of Lenovo & NVIDIA.
