Submission Guidelines

We invite submissions from all disciplines (e.g., Computer Science, Social Sciences, Engineering, Medicine, Psychology) that utilize BEAR services, High Performance Computing, and AI in their research.

Oral Presentations
  • Standard Talk: 15 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A. Ideal for completed research or significant milestones.
  • Lightning Talk: 5 minutes. Perfect for early-stage research.
  • Prizes will be awarded for "Best Talk" and "Best Lightning Talk" courtesy of Lenovo & NVIDIA.
Poster Presentations
  • Format: A1 Portrait.
  • Posters will be displayed in the Old Gym hall during lunch and coffee breaks.
  • Prizes will be awarded for "Best Poster" courtesy of Lenovo & NVIDIA.
